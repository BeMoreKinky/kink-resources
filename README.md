# My Favourite Kink Resources

Exploring kink and BDSM as a couple requires trustworthy educational resources that prioritize safety, consent, and communication. This guide presents carefully selected tools, platforms, and materials across six categories to support your journey. All resources emphasize consent-focused education and the core principles of SSC (Safe, Sane, Consensual) or RACK (Risk-Aware Consensual Kink), ensuring couples develop healthy, sustainable practices rooted in mutual respect.

## Educational Websites & Platforms

-   **BeMoreKinky** ([www.bemorekinky.com](http://www.bemorekinky.com)) - Couples-focused platform featuring interactive quizzes with 600+ activities, allowing partners to privately discover shared interests before discussing them. Includes scene planning tools and expert-written educational content.
-   **Kink Academy** ([www.kinkacademy.com](http://www.kinkacademy.com)) - Offers both a Patreon platform with monthly discussions and structured courses on power dynamics. 100% of Patreon proceeds go to social justice organizations.
-   **SADE Classes** ([www.sadeclasses.org](http://www.sadeclasses.org)) - Volunteer-run nonprofit providing vetted instructors and affordable online BDSM dynamic classes with sliding scale pricing.
-   **OhYesPlease** ([www.ohyesplease.org](http://www.ohyesplease.org)) - Playful, undogmatic approach to kink education with one-time payment courses offering lifetime access, ideal for beginners.
-   **Beducated** ([www.beducated.com](http://www.beducated.com)) - Comprehensive library of 150+ courses by world-class experts, available in four languages with a 24-hour free trial.
-   **Sexual Health Alliance** ([www.sexualhealthalliance.com](http://www.sexualhealthalliance.com)) - Professional Kink Informed Certification program with 26 hours of training and continuing education credits for healthcare providers.

## Mobile Apps & Digital Tools

## Books & Written Resources

-   **"The New Topping Book" and "The New Bottoming Book"** by Dossie Easton and Janet Hardy - Essential reading covering psychological and ethical frameworks for both dominant and submissive roles.
-   **"SM 101: A Realistic Introduction"** by Jay Wiseman - Comprehensive safety guide covering bondage, discipline, and sadomasochism techniques with meticulous attention to risk awareness.
-   **"Exploring BDSM: A Workbook for Couples Discovering Kink"** by Morgan Thorne - Interactive workbook with checklists, exercises, and guided exploration activities for partners.
-   **"Playing Well With Others"** by Lee Harrington and Mollena Williams - Addresses BDSM culture, etiquette, and social navigation for engaging with the wider kink community.

-   **SSC, RACK, and PRICK Frameworks** - Core safety philosophies explained across multiple educational sites, providing guidelines from beginner-friendly to nuanced risk awareness.
-   **"SM First Aid"** by Jay Wiseman - Emergency preparedness guide specifically for BDSM contexts with scene-specific safety protocols.
-   **Aftercare Resources** ([www.bemorekinky.com/blog](http://www.bemorekinky.com/blog)) - Comprehensive guides covering physical and emotional care, including sub/dom drop management and customized aftercare kits.
